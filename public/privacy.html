Privacy

What this site collects

Jack’s Diner’s website sets no cookies and runs no advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Nothing about your visit is stored, sold, or shared.

The contact form

When you use the contact form, you send the diner your name, your message, and — only if you choose to fill them in — your phone number and email address. The form delivers that note straight to the diner’s inbox so the team can answer you. It is used for nothing else.

The map on this site

Pages that show the location embed a Google Maps frame, which loads map tiles from Google. If you keep images disabled or block Google, the map simply won’t paint — the rest of the page works the same, and the address and directions link are right there in text.

Questions

Want something changed or deleted from a note you sent? Ask at the counter or use the contact form — say so and it’s handled.
