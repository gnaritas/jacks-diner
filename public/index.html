The story

The legend, back on George Street

For more than forty years, Charleston knew this address as Jack’s Cafe, run by Jack Sewell. This November, the name came back. Chaz Wendel and Tyler Hunt — who run Legend Deli next door — reopened the address as Jack’s Diner, an all-day diner carrying the old name into a new season.

The menu is straight diner, done right: flapjacks and corned beef hash on the grill from opening, fried chicken biscuits smothered in gravy, and plate specials running until close.

Good to know

Questions, answered

When are you open?

Every day, 7:30 a.m. to 9 p.m. Breakfast is served all day; lunch starts at 11 a.m.

Where exactly are you?

41B George St, Charleston, SC 29401 — downtown, next door to Legend Deli.

Is this the old Jack’s Cafe?

It’s the same address and the same name Charleston knew for forty years under Jack Sewell — reopened this November by Chaz Wendel and Tyler Hunt, the team behind Legend Deli next door.

What should I order?

Morning: flapjacks or corned beef hash with gravy. After 11 a.m.: a tuna melt or BLT, or come hungry for the chicken fried chicken, meatloaf, or Denver-cut steak.
